# M04 return: physical port and timing correction

The mechanical completion, calibrated live writer display and raw-to-task adapter now give the physical workstream a concrete implementation to compare with the v1 core. This comparison found a physical incompatibility that blocks the current v1 binding for the supplied removable writer: after the writer pin is removed, its inline cell is unloaded. Its 1–2 second trace cannot be labelled clay relaxation while the bridge undergoes the time-zero free-decay pitch check. Keeping that same writer engaged instead changes the acoustic boundary. Clock synchronization, a finite-reference calibration fit or renaming a column does not solve the missing force port.

There is also no measured basis for completing the writer release, 15 mm retraction, rear catch/rod-eye change, zero-load reconnection and settling inside the two seconds after the last writing ramp. Those operations change the state and must be recorded. The current task extractor therefore requires an independently qualified transition and explicit force-port identity. It refuses the detached inline writer as a permanent reaction measurement. Its synthetic traces test file/data arithmetic only.

`experiments/M04_PROTOCOL_AMENDMENT_DRAFT.json` proposes a mechanically compatible sequence without adding an imaginary reaction sensor: retain writer engaged/rod out through force windows 1–2 s, reserve a separately qualified transfer/settling slot 2–10 s, read current pitch/temperature/geometry at 10–11 s, apply the full probe at 12–14 s, decide by 15 s, retune at 15–27 s and independently score at 28–30 s. The proposed eight-second transfer is not a demonstrated time. Increase it before registration if measured operation requires more time. Longer waiting may erase fading memory; that must remain a possible negative result.

The mathematical session should own adoption and versioning of this core, its exact windows and fixed-time ledger. The present v1 core/evaluator remain unchanged in their statistical definitions; the physical-source checker now preserves the adapter's actual cost/provenance and manual-action identity. V1 command generation and extraction are runnable software references and can support an independently qualified permanent-reaction-sensor variant. They do not release M04 as a physical v1 confirmation instrument. The new physical draft is deliberately neither registered nor silently accepted by the v1 compiler.

For the revised experiment, the memoryless comparator must receive post-transfer current geometry and pitch, while the full predictor may additionally use the earlier engaged-writer force history. Otherwise a stale geometry measurement could manufacture apparent material-memory benefit. Keep the same-measurement digital comparison, fresh preparations, independent future outcome, actual action-achievement check and matched overheads. A new core must update the fixed postwrite cost total from 20 to the measured/proposed 30 seconds and retain the recording support tail as real reset overhead.

Current component corrections also matter: the compact exciter changes to the M04 4 ohm part with an E02 3.3 V amplifier supply, and E02 adds a separately calibrated room-temperature channel. Use the new geometry/configuration identifiers. Do not reuse an M03/E01 calibration identity or source waveform hash under the new assembly. No physical calibration, history effect, retention, measurement saving or speedup has been demonstrated by the software replay.
