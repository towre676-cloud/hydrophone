# Physical build handoff to Review Hidden Math

This workstream owns the M03 assembly, electrical chain, commissioning plan and executable probe/capture/file join. The other session owns the smallest useful musical decision task, its write histories, matched-current-condition requirements, admissible actions, observation bounds, comparison baselines and preregistered claim criteria. The meeting point is `hardware/calibration/experiment_interface.json`, with actual raw records in canonical `fusion.run/1`; the original archived handoff remains available but is superseded for current build coordinates by M03.

Four actual recovered Gen2 tuning targets feed one common exciter. Four strings are manually tuned; the prototype does not independently retune eight software voices or seventeen spellings. The stationary probe is distinct from musical audition. Its repeatable electrical command, common-clock hydrophone/bridge-acceleration/loopback channels and separately synchronized writer data supply measured features. A digital drive setting is not force, and the writer load cell does not measure audio input force.

Initial mechanical commissioning uses the first of 5 N or 0.25 mm writer motion with the water rod disconnected, and no more than 10 micrometres actually measured diaphragm reader motion about wet equilibrium. These are provisional ceilings subject to installed checks, not calibrated released ratings. Geometry, slip, temperature, preload, water depth, sensor gain/phase and read disturbance must be recorded. Fixed-coefficient SLS acoustic transfer is independent of its initial internal memory coordinate, so a history-dependent decision cannot be justified by simply assigning different initial SLS states. The task must identify a measurable history effect or report that it was not demonstrated.

The acquisition software issues no tuning/slow-writing actions and never silently converts diagnostic residuals into certified observation intervals. Before a Fusion decision can be physically acted upon, the other session must provide an explicit adapter from the measured/calibrated features to its finite state/action contract, a justified uncertainty model, and a defined response when observations are ambiguous or outside the declared domain. Keep the original recovered engines and the separate twelve-pitch-class proxy distinguishable.

The new synthetic commissioning replay checks the mechanics of data exchange, nonzero clock drift, raw-marker evidence and feature recovery. It has zero hardware commands and zero physical measurements. It is not evidence of retained clay memory, useful computation, sensor reduction or speedup. The final experiment must compare a memoryless predictor and a digital estimator given the same observations, including measurement/preparation costs appropriate to the task.
