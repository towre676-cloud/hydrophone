# M04 completion: nominal machining and state changes

M04 replaces the unfinished mechanical interfaces with the dimensioned parts in `completion.json`, the cut/drill/turning tables and the two SCAD files. The retained M03 files are immutable. Coordinates below are global millimetres unless explicitly called local. Commercial bodies remain maker-controlled parts, not shapes to manufacture from our envelopes. Physical acceptance remains a measured commissioning operation; nominal part dimensions no longer depend on an unspecified connector blank.

The first write limit remains **±5 N or ±0.25 mm, whichever comes first**, with live calibrated force and independent after-cell carrier displacement visible. The read criterion remains **≤10 µm actual dynamic diaphragm displacement about wet equilibrium**. Catch contact terminates a run. The conditional 20 N/0.5 mm arithmetic case does not authorize operation, and neither the catches nor hand-screw torque limit force automatically.

## Exact manual write-to-read transition

Start from a recorded state with **rear water rod removed**, flat catch target installed, writer front pin engaged, stops set, hand display live, and raw force/displacement recording. Complete the prescribed ramp/hold/unload while preserving the raw DC baseline. Record the actual end time. Latch/hold the manual screw only as the protocol specifies; do not call a subsequent material relaxation a zeroing correction.

Remove the writer front-pin retainer and pin without dragging the carrier, then retract the writer15 mm and confirm a visible gap. Record that operation and the changed force path. Remove the rear flat target/pin, insert the water-eye/rod, fit its spacers/pin, set the wet-zero turnbuckle and lock it while checking carrier position, preload and bore clearance. Record every step's start/end and the measured settling/dwell. Verify the proper mode, absence of catch contact, gain/sensor state and valid dynamic displacement conversion before a read. If a step changes residual geometry or stress outside the registered preparation, the attempted comparison has changed its physical state.

**No transition time has been measured. These manual operations are not instantaneous and are not claimed to fit a2 s task window.** The present history core that ends with1 s cycle rest plus1 s end rest cannot be registered for this apparatus merely by assigning read time zero.
