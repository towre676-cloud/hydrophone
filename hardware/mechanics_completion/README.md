# M04 manual completion kit

M04 completes the nominal custom parts that M03 left as interfaces: a bidirectional captive lead screw with opposed thrust bearings, both miniature force-cell connectors, aligned carrier travel catches, a fine zero-load water rod, line retainers/tension adjustment, a hinged guard and a smaller exciter mount. The old `hardware/mechanics/` M03 files remain unchanged. This is a machining design and replayable geometry/load check; no component has been fabricated or qualified.

`completion.json` is the dimensional contract. `parts.scad` supplies the M04 modules and individual part selectors. `assembly.scad` combines retained M03 modules with the completion kit. `build.py` writes nominal cut/drill tables and arithmetic/clearance receipts; `drawing.py` writes the axial machining sheet. The `retainers_guard/` subfolder supplies the string, guard and exciter detail. All dimensions are millimetres, with the old base coordinates retained. The front outrigger and right tuner/guard extend the benchtop envelope; the parked hand knob reaches Y = −140 and the guard reaches X = 625. This extension is real hardware, not a change of drawing scale.

The force-cell body now occupies load stations **Y = 5…24.1**, with the same X = 460/Z = 92 axis. This creates space for actual 18.5 mm spherical ends. The two shouldered M3 studs each engage the cell by 2.2 mm, below its 2.8 mm maximum, while engaging the igus rod end by 5 mm. The rear pin is Y = −18.9; the existing front instrument pin remains Y = 48. Lead-screw torque is reacted by a fixed bronze nut and opposed **EZO F6-12M 6 × 12 × 4.5 mm thrust bearings**, never by screwing the sensor body around.

The central catches act on a 6 mm target at the rear clevis **(460,110,92)**, keeping stop force on the same transverse line instead of twisting the carrier at an outer edge. The hollow rear catch clears the 3 mm water rod. At ±0.30 mm initial catch gaps, the ±0.25 mm write envelope remains inside the catches. Stop contact still terminates the experiment. Writing begins only with the rod disconnected, calibrated live force and after-cell displacement visible, and the original ±5 N/±0.25 mm first limit intact. No motor control is added.

The corrected small exciter is the **Dayton DAEX-13-4SM, 3 W/4 Ω**, not the much larger DAEX25. The maker's dimensioned specification and purchased revision govern its envelope; the mount allows at least 20.2 × 41 × 9 mm. Electrical power and optical/acceleration conversion remain separate calibrated boundaries. The actual diaphragm-motion limit of 10 µm about wet equilibrium remains unqualified until measured; a digital command does not enforce it.

Use `ASSEMBLY.md` for exact turning stations, fits, engagement, assembly sequence and acceptance criteria, and `sources.json` for the primary component documents. No purchase, deployment or physical driver is included.
