> **Research history.** [E01 README.md](README.md) and [channel_plan.json](channel_plan.json) govern current candidate wiring and settings. The current M03 base is 600 × 360 mm; older 600 × 320 mm dimensions below describe the historical alternative, not the current build.

Manufacturer sources checked **24 October 2026**. These are unpurchased candidates for the dry writing fixture on the 600 × 320 mm instrument. The physical-build baseline now requires an inline bidirectional cell and non-contact displacement, reviewed in the electronics owner's main document. The Phidgets/indicator chain below is a lower-cost, slow-hold alternative, not that baseline. None of its parts measures audio-frequency bridge displacement.

## Inline bidirectional baseline: FUTEK and InAmp3 findings

FUTEK's live primary drawing **FI1041-G**, served as [fsh00103.pdf](https://media.futek.com/content/futek/files/pdf/productdrawings/fsh00103.pdf), identifies **LSB200 FSH00103** as 5 lb / 22.2 N, M3 × 0.5 threads, nominal 2 mV/V and 350 Ω. Full-scale deflection is 0.005 in (0.127 mm); listed natural frequency is 1,900 Hz. Excitation maximum is 10 V; calibration test excitation is 5 V. Nonlinearity and hysteresis are each ±0.1% of rated output; nonrepeatability is ±0.05%. Standard calibration is five-point tension; compression calibration is separately available. A 20 N excursion is close to rated capacity, and the broad manufacturer error listing does not establish 0.01 N absolute force accuracy. Installed calibration of the intended ±5 N initial band is required. Current availability and manufacturer price were not verified.

The FUTEK wiring code is **red +excitation, black −excitation, green +signal, white −signal; shield floating**. Do not reuse the Phidgets cell's shield-to-ground instruction for this cell. Apply loads only through the designated ends, leaving nonloading surfaces clear. The neighboring FSH00102 is 2 lb / 8.9 N with 0.004 in rated deflection; it cannot support the proposed conditional 20 N range. Cell compliance is comparable to the initial small write strokes: a micrometer or stage encoder upstream of the cell cannot alone establish clay displacement.

The current [LJTick-InAmp3 datasheet](https://support.labjack.com/docs/ljtick-inamp-3-0-datasheet) specifies fixed **1.25 V output offset**, 11×/51×/201× gain variants set by resistors, and a 2.50 V reference rated to 20 mA. This is not the discontinued DIP-switch InAmp2. The proposed 350 Ω cell draws approximately 7.14 mA from 2.5 V excitation. Connect red to `VREF`, black to `GND`, green to `INA+`, and white to `INA-`; retain the shield treatment above. For the current E01 candidate, use a labelled breakout for **VS/GND power only**; route **OUTA → external 3364 channel 1, DC-coupled 5 Hz low-pass → T7 AIN0**. Do not directly connect OUTA to AIN0, bypass or short the external filter, or mate the module's output pins directly into the T7 analog-input terminals. Leave OUTB disconnected from AIN1. The authoritative channel plan governs the complete connection and filter settings.

At 201× gain, ideal full-scale output is approximately 0.245–2.255 V. The documented lower output limit is 0.2 V, leaving little negative-range margin; published gain/offset limits and actual zero must be checked before loading. The initial ±5 N range is approximately 1.024–1.476 V. Use 51× if the wider bidirectional range cannot retain measured headroom. The module does not supply force calibration or anti-alias certification. Its [primary product page](https://labjack.com/products/ljtick-inamp3) displayed **US $75**; no purchase was made.

## Alternative slow-hold components and connections

Use a **Phidgets FRC4125_0, 3 kg C4 single-point load cell**, a **DAQ1500_0 bridge interface**, and a **HUB0002_1 USB-C VINT Hub** for force. Use a **Mitutoyo 543-700B standard ID-C indicator** with the current **06AGQ001F USB-ITN-SF, 2 m** input tool for independently measured, slow displacement. The indicator is a separate USB stream: it is not synchronized to the bridge ADC by sharing a computer. A rigid dry bracket holds the indicator body; its tip observes the moving writer/bridge reference. Retract it before free acoustic readings.

The following is an exact electrical map for the named force components, following the load-cell manufacturer's connection table. Connect FRC4125 red to DAQ1500 channel 0 `5V`, green to `+`, white to `-`, black to `G`, and silver shield/earth to `G`. Use the supplied 3-wire VINT cable between DAQ1500 and hub port 0. Connect the hub's USB-C port to the logging computer. The bridge receives excitation from DAQ1500; do not also connect a bench excitation supply. DAQ1500 channel 1 remains disabled in the baseline. The USB power budget and the mechanical cable strain relief must be checked on the assembled fixture. [S1,S2,S3]

Connect the **543-700B's specified current-generation output connector** to the matching end of **06AGQ001F**, then connect its USB plug to the logging computer. The indicator uses its CR2032 battery; the interface cable receives USB power. Do not substitute older 06AFM380F/905338 combinations solely because another indicator also says “ID-C”: the current catalog explicitly changes connector shape. USB-ITPAK V3.0 is the catalog's optional collection/control software; its full functionality has a separate licensing dongle. The cable can act as keyboard HID, but that does not establish a uniformly sampled stream or a hardware acquisition timestamp. [S4,S5]

The FRC4125 is a **single-point weighing cell**, not an off-the-shelf bidirectional axial force transducer. Its specified load direction, one-end support, clearance under the flexing portion, and opposing mounting points must follow the actual mechanical drawing. The mechanical owner must supply a calibrated load-axis fixture and limit stops. Do not assume that a sideways load, mounting moment, reversed load, or force indicated at the screw is equal to clay shear force. Baseline the writer, guide and indicator loads with a reference cartridge before interpreting material force.

## Clock and acquisition contract

The computer should retain three distinct time domains: audio sample frames, bridge-interface callbacks, and indicator read transactions. Record raw bridge voltage ratio, chosen gain/data interval, the locally monotonic receive timestamp, and converted force. For indicator reads, record the monotonic request and receipt times, raw indicated length, resolution, unit and any origin/reset event. A request–receipt interval is preferable to falsely assigning an exact capture epoch. There is **no verified manufacturer guarantee of 50 Hz simultaneous force/displacement output** in this candidate chain.

Use a repeated identifiable synchronization marker at the beginning and end, plus intermediate markers for long holds. The slow-side electrical requirement is an **isolated dry-contact/open-collector closure** from a reserved hub port's signal to its ground, held at least 100 ms as a design choice above the published 3 ms filter minimum. The audio-chain owner should record the same physical marker through a separately conditioned audio channel. Do not connect a hub logic output or 5 V rail directly to an audio microphone input. Shared operator commands do not prove simultaneous edges: measure the marker branch skew, USB/ADC latency and repeatability before fitting the affine clock map. Preserve fitted offset, drift and residual, and reject observations whose admissible timing interval exceeds the experiment's predeclared tolerance. This marker yields identifiable correspondence, **not** audio-clock synchronization of the slow ADC.

Start with step-and-hold mechanics and deliberately slow motion. The DAQ1500 product page does not establish a complete installed anti-alias transfer specification. Its 50 sample/s limit does not by itself certify usable response through 25 Hz or reject vibration aliases. Measure settling and response at the configured gain/rate; do not infer sharp force spikes from undersampled data. Retract the indicator and release its coupling for acoustic reads. Check whether reconnecting it changes the retained state.

For work estimates, preserve the actual irregular time sequence and measured positions. Do not resample indicator values into a fictitious 50 Hz position trace or pair a force sample with an unqualified position epoch. The current sampled-path computation can operate after deliberate paired records are produced; full continuous work still needs bounded motion between samples.

## Lower-cost displacement parts considered, with explicit limits

Phidgets **3579_0** offers 75 mm electrical travel, 80 mm mechanical travel, 5 kΩ resistance, 180 g mass and **0.02%** listed repeatability: interpreted over its electrical span, this is 15 µm. It therefore does not establish the proposed 10 µm displacement target. The **3580_0** has 100 mm electrical travel, 105 mm physical travel, 5 kΩ, 208 g and **0.01%** listed repeatability: 10 µm under the same interpretation. Neither listing supplies an installed absolute-accuracy or contact-friction guarantee. Both connect through the supplied Phidget cable adapter to a hub port in voltage-ratio mode. Body travel, drag, electrical noise, calibration and mechanical fit must be assessed before choosing them for a submillimetre clay experiment. They can provide inexpensive exploratory relative position, but are not substitutes for confirmed displacement metrology. [S6,S7]
