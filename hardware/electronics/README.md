# E01: candidate sensing, drive and clock chain

Manufacturer material was checked on **24 October 2026**. This is an unpurchased bench-build specification for the M03 600 × 360 mm four-string/clay/water instrument (base Y=−40..320 mm, extended for the manual writer). It defines electrical connections and commissioning measurements; it is not an assembled system, a calibrated force source, or authorization to infer SI quantities from nominal sensitivity. The exact manufacturer URLs and retrieval limitations are in [sources.json](sources.json), with candidate quantities and verified prices in [bom.csv](bom.csv). The original engineering packet is unchanged.

## Baseline and physical packaging

Use a **Behringer UMC404HD** as the four-input common audio-clock device at **48,000 samples/s**, with all gains fixed and photographed. Keep the interface, conditioner, filter and computer on a dry adjacent bench, not underneath the water cell. A **PCB 352C33** on the dry moving carrier measures acceleration along the shear direction. Its body/cable/mount mass changes the moving system: document the mounted configuration and repeat a reference response after mounting. A stationary **Micro-Epsilon ILD1420-10CL1** observes a diffuse target on the moving clay plate/carrier near the deformation plane, approximately 25 mm away, along Y. The optical head adds no moving sensor mass. One optical point does not independently resolve carrier translation and pitch.

The independent slow force line uses **FUTEK LSB200 FSH00103**, **LabJack T7** and a current **LJTick-InAmp 3 configured for gain 201**. The cell is inline in the Y writer load path at the mechanical drawing's Z=92 mm force line. Its two M3 × 0.5 load threads are only 2.8 mm deep; use the manufacturer's drawing, keep nonloading surfaces clear, and prevent thread bottoming or torque through the flexure. Initial mechanics commissioning is limited to the mechanical owner's ±5 N/±0.25 mm envelope. Manual writing additionally requires an installed, calibrated live force/plate-position display that the operator can read while moving the hand stage; this batch logger supplies no such display or automatic limit control. Wider ±20 N/±0.5 mm operation requires a separate installed check. Cell compliance means upstream screw travel cannot substitute for measured plate motion.

The baseline includes one external four-channel **Krohn-Hite 3364** filter: two DC-coupled channels at 5 Hz for the slow force/displacement voltages and two at 2 kHz for audio sensor paths. Both selections use Butterworth low-pass, 0 dB input gain and 0 dB output gain. This is bench instrumentation, approximately 36 × 32 cm, not a hidden enclosure-sized part or a verified inexpensive bill of materials. A compact replacement requires its own verified noise, DC drift, passband, stopband and delay specification.

## Exact audio connection map

| Physical input / saved zero-based channel | Name | Connection |
|---|---|---|
| IN1 / 0 | `hydrophone_raw` | Aquarian H2dX factory XLR → UMC IN1 XLR. Enable the interface's +48 V phantom supply only after connecting the matched hydrophone. H2dX pin 1 is ground, pin 2 signal and pin 3 unused; do not rewire it into a different microphone convention. |
| IN2 / 1 | `bridge_acceleration_raw` | 352C33 → PCB 003C10 10-foot 10-32-coax-to-BNC sensor cable → PCB 480E09 `SENSOR` input. Its `OUTPUT` → filter CH3 input; filter CH3 output → UMC IN2 quarter-inch LINE input. A BNC-to-TS lead connects center to tip and shield to sleeve. |
| IN3 / 2 | `drive_loopback_raw` | Passive parallel tap of the actual PLAYBACK A 1/L output, before the drive isolator → differential resistor pad below → IN3 quarter-inch LINE input. This is the electrical DAC command reference, not coil voltage, current or force. |
| IN4 / 3 | `sync_marker_raw` | Conditioned LabJack DAC0 marker node described below → IN4 quarter-inch LINE input. OUT2 is silent and has no electrical connection to this node. |

Use quarter-inch LINE connections for IN2–IN4, not XLR adapters. The current U-PHORIA guide lists the combo inputs, phantom switch and line levels but does not provide a connector-level DC-isolation schematic. Before attaching the conditioner or logger, verify on the actual unit that enabling phantom does not put DC on these quarter-inch signal contacts. Unexpected DC is a commissioning failure, not something to compensate in software. No unused XLR input is connected to an instrument voltage source. IN2–IN4 LINE/INST switches stay at LINE. Begin with gain minimum and pads engaged; document any subsequent change and repeat the electrical channel calibration.

For the hydrophone anti-alias path, use the UMC404HD **INSERT 1** connector: the conventional insert lead's send/tip goes to filter CH4 input and filter CH4 output returns through the insert return/ring; sleeve is the signal reference. Verify this send/return convention against the actual cable and unit with an electrical loopback before applying phantom. This places the external filter after the microphone preamplifier and before digitization. Leave inserts 2–4 unconnected. Preserve the insert/filter path in the hydrophone calibration identifier. The 3364's 1 MΩ input also avoids loading the 480E09 directly with an unspecified low-impedance LINE load; its filtered output drives IN2.

The recorder's four named channels must remain in the table's physical order. Capture all four in one duplex stream; operating-system sound enhancements, automatic gain, resampling and monitoring-to-output feedback are disabled. The manufacturer specifies higher converter capability, but this pipeline's PCM16 archive is a distinct format choice and has a 16-bit quantization floor. Check actual callback status, dropped frames and sample rate. A common audio device does not certify zero interchannel analog phase error.

Build the IN3 pad as two 10.0 kΩ, 1% series resistors, one from the tapped output tip to IN3 tip and one from tapped ring to IN3 ring; connect 2.21 kΩ, 1% across the **IN3-side** tip and ring. Connect sleeve to sleeve, and insulate the junctions in the labelled box. The calculated unloaded differential voltage ratio is 2.21/(10+10+2.21) = 0.09950, approximately −20.04 dB. The unknown actual LINE input impedance changes that ratio; calibrate the loaded complete path and verify that both parallel branches together do not overload the output. Do not join either signal leg to sleeve or join two outputs. Retain this attenuator in the configuration record; its loss also reduces usable PCM16 reference resolution.

## Isolated low-voltage actuator path

Route the same **PLAYBACK A 1/L** output used by the loopback through **Radial J-Iso channel 1**, then into a **SparkFun BOB-11044 TPA2005D1** mono amplifier. Use a TRS-to-XLR male balanced lead: tip→pin 2, ring→pin 3, sleeve→pin 1. J-Iso channel 1 unbalanced output center/tip→amplifier `IN+`; output return→`IN-` and amplifier signal ground. J-Iso provides transformer isolation in the audio drive path; it does not make all sensor/logger/USB grounds isolated. Its level control is recorded and initially at minimum. Channel 2 is unused.

The SparkFun manufacturer's source schematic identifies **JP1 pin 1 = SDN, pin 2 = VCC, pin 3 = GND; JP2 pin 1 = IN+, pin 2 = IN−; JP3 pin 1 = OUT+, pin 2 = OUT−**. Check the purchased board revision and printed labels before terminating wires. Supply the board with a separate regulated **5 V** source, for example **Mean Well GST25A05-P1J**, via its matched center-positive barrel breakout, an accessible DC power switch and a 1 A fuse. The supply's larger available current is not an exciter operating limit. Wire a separate labelled MUTE switch between SDN and GND; closed means muted. Leave gain resistors and volume solder jumper at their manufacturer configuration initially.

Connect JP3 OUT+ and OUT− only to the two terminals of **one Dayton DAEX25, 8 Ω**. Neither class-D output terminal is ground. Do not connect either output to an audio input, USB ground or the hydrophone circuit. Keep the twisted speaker pair short and separated from sensor leads. Mount the exciter dry on a removable mechanical drive tab designed by the mechanical owner; it is an inertial surface exciter, not a force-controlled modal shaker. It must not bridge the moving guide to the fixed frame. Its adhesive joint, added mass and coupling transfer are measured parts of the fixture.

The source-listed amplifier capability is 1.4 W into 8 Ω; the exciter listing is 5 W RMS. Neither number permits that drive on this cartridge. First test the amplifier on an 8 Ω, at least 5 W dummy resistor, with a differential probe or properly isolated measurement across OUT+/OUT−. Begin below a **measured 0.1 W electrical** trial level, and reduce it further when connecting the instrument. This is an electrical starting ceiling, not a verified mechanical envelope. Record actual loaded gain, distortion, coil-terminal voltage/current if available, and bridge motion. The low-voltage supply switch and MUTE remain effective independently of software. The delivered capture plan is a voltage-command experiment; it has no dynamic force sensor.

Use the hardware stream list `[AIN0, AIN2, AIN4, AIN6]`, all ±10 V, resolution index 7. **50 scans/s** is the initial declared setting; 200 scans/s is an optional later configuration below the published four-channel limit. These channels are sequential, with approximately 670 µs interchannel conversion spacing at this setting, not simultaneous. Record scan list/order, actual accepted rate, first-scan epoch convention, interchannel offsets and stream loss indicators. Reconstruct logger times from sample indices and the hardware stream rate; USB callback arrival timestamps are transport diagnostics.

The optical output's 1–5 V span, class-1 model's 12-bit analog conversion and specified surface conditions must be retained. Read the **actual moving plate**, not the writer stage behind the compliant force cell. The 5 lb cell's published nonlinearity alone exceeds 0.01 N across its full range; ADC codes and repeatability are not absolute accuracy. At 201× gain and 2.5 V excitation the calculated nominal force slope is about 45.3 mV/N. The full negative range approaches amplifier lower headroom; qualify the ±5 N installed range first and use a lower-gain configuration if headroom fails. Both load directions, guide reaction, cable forces, mounting compliance, optical datum and filter DC drift require calibration. See the two research notes for the verified facts and limited alternative components.

## Executable slow-voltage capture

[`t7_capture.py`](t7_capture.py) uses the official optional LJM Python wrapper and the host's separately installed native LabJack LJM library. The default command reads no device and imports no SDK:

```sh
python hardware/electronics/t7_capture.py --duration 30
```

After the electrical checkout, an operator explicitly selects the actual T7 serial to acquire the four raw voltages and command only the conditioned DAC0 marker:

```sh
python hardware/electronics/t7_capture.py --execute --identifier SERIAL --duration 30 --output-dir results/t7_slow_001
```

Replace `SERIAL` with the numeric device serial; a missing or mismatched selection is rejected. Do not reuse an output directory containing capture files. The script writes `raw_slow_volts.csv`, `t7_blocks.jsonl` and `t7_capture.json`, retaining device-calibrated voltages, all `-9999` skipped-data sentinels, per-block backlogs and byte hashes. It reconstructs `time_s` from scan index divided by the **actual** accepted hardware scan rate. Its output is neither force in newtons nor displacement in metres. Convert a copy through separately validated installed calibration, retain the voltage CSV unchanged, and import only the resulting explicitly calibrated columns. Marker commands and USB return times remain diagnostics; actual recorded AIN4/IN4 edges establish alignment. A capture receipt does not assert valid alignment or physical readiness.

Run its hardware-free mocked integration checks with `python -m unittest discover -s hardware/electronics -p 'test_t7_capture.py' -v`. These checks exercise the adapter contract; they do not validate a real ADC, timing path or sensor.

The CSV and final receipt cannot enforce the writer's ±5 N/±0.25 mm limits. Before any manual write, provide a separate commissioned live display using the installed signed force and actual plate-position calibrations, verify display latency/settling, and establish a hand-step/check/dwell procedure that stays within the mechanical envelope. A display must consume this stream or its live file without competing for a second T7 configuration/stream owner. Until that display and procedure exist, manual writing is blocked. Raw volts, post-run SI conversion and software exception handling are not real-time force protection; this code has no emergency-stop function.

## Physical marker and two independent clock maps

Use **T7 DAC0 only** as the slow/audio marker source. Command approximately 0.05→1.00 V pulses with at least **300 ms high and 300 ms low**, in a distinctive nonuniform sequence before, during and after the record. Wire DAC0 directly to AIN4 so the actual local voltage, including loading and command jitter, is recorded. For the audio branch, connect DAC0 through **10.0 kΩ** to node M; connect **2.21 kΩ** from M to T7 GND; connect M through a **10 µF nonpolar, ≥25 V capacitor** to IN4 tip. IN4 ring and sleeve return to the logger/audio reference. This is a custom passive conditioning box, not a manufacturer-calibrated pulse source. Verify component values, maximum voltage, leakage, ground noise and the entire edge response before the instrument is connected. Its unloaded divider ratio is a calculated 0.181; actual LINE loading and coupling change the response. A plateau becomes AC-coupled transients on the audio input.

The slow pick is bracketed by the last pre-edge and first post-edge stream samples. At 50 scans/s, budget at least **20 ms** absent separately verified interpolation, then add channel-scan skew, detector/path uncertainty and fitted clock-map residual. Do not replace that allowance with an audio-sample interval. Fit logger→audio offset and drift from multiple corresponding physical edges and retain their bracketing intervals. This clock map does not remove the force/laser filters’ physical group delay: the nominal four-pole 5 Hz Butterworth delay is about 83 ms near DC and about 90 ms at 2 Hz. Record measured channel-specific transfer/delay and conversion offsets, or use separately justified settled quasistatic windows; do not label filtered samples as undelayed material snapshots. Separately, correlate at least three identifiable generated DAC-waveform segments with IN3 electrical loopback to estimate command→audio timing. These are different maps; OUT2 remains silent and no two voltage sources are wired together. Reject an experiment whose combined timing interval exceeds its predeclared tolerance.

## Filtering and bench release

The filter settings are engineering selections for initial **65–1,000 Hz audio** and **up to 2 Hz slow mechanics**. The UMC guide does not publish enough ADC stopband/phase information to substitute its maximum sample rate for an anti-alias certificate. External filters also have gain, phase, offset and finite rejection. Measure complete channel transfer, out-of-band pickup and alias rejection with the installed settings. In particular, the class-D stage switches near 250 kHz; transformer isolation and a nominal audio sample rate do not prove that RF pickup or nonlinear demodulation is absent. At 50 Hz slow sampling, a 5 Hz four-pole filter is not an unlimited bound on every possible out-of-band disturbance. A finite spectral/drive-domain validity statement and measured disturbance checks remain required.

The first release is **bench checkout**, with the exciter disconnected from the object: inspect supply polarity and sensor bias, verify phantom isolation at the actual jacks, calibrate all electrical input paths with the same reference signal, measure loopback and marker maps, check the dummy-load amplifier, and retain clipping/loss/status records. Then attach the dry mechanical coupon at the lowest observed drive, qualify force/displacement baselines and travel/force stops, and repeat dry/empty-cell/filled-cell reference tests. End-to-end per-tone pressure and acceleration conversions remain locked until their measured complex sensitivities and bounds exist. Static load calibration does not identify dynamic actuator force, and sampled writer-port work does not become clay dissipation. No live purchase, wiring, calibration or hardware test has occurred in this workstream.

The 50 Hz, 5 Hz-filtered slow optical channel cannot measure the approximately 300 Hz diaphragm excursion and cannot enforce a 10 micrometre acoustic-read limit. The initial acoustic read therefore stays blocked pending an independently qualified dynamic displacement measurement or a measured bridge-to-diaphragm transfer with a conservative uncertainty bound. Moving the laser to an indexed diaphragm/rod target for a separate high-rate commissioning test is a possible later configuration, but its analog bandwidth, sample rate, local dynamic calibration, target geometry and uncertainty must be qualified first. The nominal 8 micrometre full-span optical linearity listing is not a 10 micrometre dynamic-amplitude certificate.
