# E02 integration with the M04 completion kit

E02 is the current integration overlay for `mechanics_completion/`, `readout/`, `commissioning/` and the shared clay-history tuning experiment. M03 and E01 remain historical, reproducible design checkpoints. Their dimensions, drive component and power settings must not silently carry into M04/E02. No components have been purchased, assembled or physically qualified by this release.

## Corrected drive component and supply

The DAEX25's 25 mm designation is its voice-coil size; it is not a 25 mm outer mounting envelope. Replace E01's DAEX25 with **Dayton DAEX-13-4SM**, using the completion kit's full purchased-revision keepout and removable vertical drive vane. Dayton's product page lists 18.5 × 38.5 × 8 mm and 6.7 g, while the linked drawing contains larger dimensions; the completion drawing uses the conservative envelope and requires inspection of the supplied revision. The manufacturer's 4 ohm, 3 W listing does not establish installed force or a permitted experiment power. Its uncoupled resonance is not a calibrated operating band.

The existing SparkFun BOB-11044 uses TI's TPA2005D1. TI permits the 4 ohm load only over a 2.5–4.2 V supply range. **Do not connect the old 5 V supply directly to this amplifier with the new exciter.** Retain the sealed external 5 V supply upstream, then connect it through the existing fuse and accessible DC switch to a **Pololu D24V10F3, item 2830**: +5 V to VIN, return to GND; VOUT to amplifier VCC, regulator GND to amplifier GND. The 3.3 V regulator's listed 4% output accuracy corresponds to 3.168–3.432 V under its stated scope, inside TI's 4 ohm range. This calculation is not a measurement of startup overshoot, actual loaded rail or a regulator fault guarantee. Verify polarity and loaded/startup behavior on the bench before attaching the exciter. The board has no reverse-polarity protection.

Retain the isolated audio input, differential floating class-D output, independent SDN-to-GND MUTE switch and short twisted speaker pair. OUT+ and OUT− go only to the exciter terminals. Replace the 8 ohm bench dummy with a **4 ohm, at least 5 W dummy load**; verify its resistance and thermal suitability. Begin below the prior measured 0.1 W electrical checkout ceiling, reducing drive further for installed-motion qualification. Neither the amplifier's maximum nor the exciter's 3 W listing authorizes that power on the clay/water mechanism. Regulator switching noise, amplifier switching noise and actual mounting transfer belong in the installed electrical/mechanical qualification.

## Room-temperature channel for the shared task

The other session's experiment requires a measured room-temperature mean during seconds 1–2. E01's four slow channels did not include a room thermometer. E02 adds **Analog Devices TMP36GT9Z, TO-92**, to **T7 AIN8**, keeping force, position, marker and excitation monitor unchanged. Identify pins using the manufacturer's **bottom-view** drawing: pin 1 +VS, pin 2 VOUT, pin 3 GND. Connect +VS to the T7 sensor supply, GND to the logger signal reference, and VOUT to AIN8 through a documented local low-pass network. A concrete starting network is 1.00 kilohm series and 100 microfarads shunt to GND at AIN8, with 100 nanofarads local supply decoupling; the requested network is an engineering selection to characterize, including capacitor leakage, loading, actual response and temperature drift. Keep all supply voltages inside the sensor's specified range.

Mount the room sensor on the fixed dry frame in free room air, shaded from direct radiant sources, away from the amplifier/regulator and the tank's splash zone. Record its position and thermal settling. It measures room air only after that exposure has been qualified; it does not stand in for water temperature. Preserve raw `raw_temperature_V`. The manufacturer's nominal 10 mV/degree and offset are not installed calibration. Use independent temperature references and the commissioning fitter's optional `temperature_C` channel to produce a scoped derivative and bound. No physical episode may fill in a default room temperature.

Run `t7_capture.py --profile E02` to stream `[AIN0, AIN2, AIN4, AIN6, AIN8]` at the requested 50 scans/s, range ±10 V, resolution index 7. The default remains E01 for replay compatibility. Accepted rate and all five sequential-channel offsets must be qualified and retained. The E01 four-channel timing listing is not automatically a five-channel guarantee. The instrument's audio channels remain IN1 hydrophone, IN2 bridge acceleration, IN3 electrical loopback and IN4 marker.

## Writer display and commissioning evidence

`readout/writer_display.py` attaches to the **same** T7 stream through start/block/end observations; it never opens a second ADC handle. A watchdog can display STOP when reads stall. Raw native voltage files remain unchanged. Its SI display requires installed serial/configuration-bound calibration, both signed load directions, applicable environment/excitation domains, bounded sample age, filter/scan delays and an independently justified slew allowance. A display-only tare cannot change the absolute force/displacement limit checks. Unknown calibration, age, scope or error is a STOP state. This is an operator aid, not a hard real-time controller or an automatic protective stop.

`commissioning/` supplies reference fitting, held-out checks and conditional dynamic boss-motion envelopes. Finite calibration residuals are empirical evidence; they are not uniform bounds over unmeasured conditions. Independently justify the stated complete error budgets before enabling physical scope. The motion record must say whether it bounds one boss coordinate or the entire diaphragm; a one-point sensor cannot establish an unobserved spatial maximum. Mechanical catches and the installed independent checks remain necessary.

## Exact shared experiment timeline

Use `python -m instrument episode-commands experiments/clay_tuning_v1/contract.json --output-dir results/episode_commands_001`. It renders the other session's exact two-second probe, embeds it at 2–4 s in the full stereo command, and writes a separate reduced silent command. OUT2 remains silent. Register the complete played-command SHA256 for each arm. `load_probe` checks the actual complete bytes, source-renderer replay, core digest, slot and omission before capture; the ten-second engineering probe cannot substitute for these files.

The task endpoint remains 20 s. Recording continues to 21 s to support a nonzero timing bound at the right edge; charge that extra second explicitly to `reset_s` and retain matched overheads. Pre-action pitch requires a separately qualified free-decay pluck/read, and outcome pitch requires its own later read. A driven E4 line is not natural string pitch. The reduced record still retains loopback/marker acquisition and checks actual omission. Silence cannot supply waveform-correlation clock anchors: use separately qualified current-episode timing evidence rather than manufacturing anchors from zeros.

`instrument/task_features.py` replays hash-bound raw records into the other session's exact feature windows and separate outcome record. It is an offline replay adapter; its output cannot prove that an operator/computer made a decision before the past deadline. Qualification on the target computer must establish live acquisition/extraction/decision latency, manual retune achievement, read disturbance and all source/time bounds. Omission checking ends at 4 s even when reduced feature values end at 2 s; this fixed-epoch task claims no elapsed-time saving from omitting the probe. Failed or unresolved assigned episodes remain operational failures rather than being silently excluded.
