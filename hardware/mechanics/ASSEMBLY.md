# M03 fabrication, assembly and qualification

M03 is a manually operated coupon instrument with a common bridge. These are fabrication-review instructions, not a claim that the assembled design has passed. The original 600 × 320 packet remains unchanged; this revision uses the actual 600 × 360 base needed by the writer, wider leaf spacing, a 100 mm afterlength and aligned Z = 92 force ports. Use the **M03 parameter hash and drawing together**. Parts made from the earlier drawing cannot silently inherit M03 calculations.

## Manufacturing datums and tolerances

The base datum is its top plane Z = 12; X = 0 is its left edge and Y = 0 lies 40 mm behind the new front edge. Thus the base occupies X = 0…600, Y = −40…320. `generated/drill_table.csv` uses **part-local installed X/Y/Z axes**: the base local Y is global Y + 40. A hole's axis, local start coordinate, pilot/clearance diameter, thread and depth are separate columns. For annular parts, the stock origin is the low X/Z corner of the 60 × 60 bounding square; their centre is local (30,30). Do not interpret a tap-drill diameter as a clearance hole. A tapped hole located at the top face runs downward into the stated depth. Avoid drilling through to an opposite feature.

Machine the fixed datum faces square and parallel to 0.05 mm over each part. Locate structural mounting holes to ±0.10 mm, cartridge registration and clamp edges to ±0.05 mm, and match the four 18 mm spacers within 0.02 mm. These are **requested shop tolerances**, not measured capabilities or a metrology uncertainty budget. Measure finished parts and re-enter actual dimensions before interpreting displacement or the stress screen. Leaf free span is set by the clamp edges, not by the 60 mm blank length. Burrs, scored roots or clamp fretting invalidate the ideal-beam calculation. No finite-element/fatigue analysis or stress-concentration certificate accompanies this packet.

Ordered spring stock is 0.012 in **0.3048 mm full-hard 302 stainless**, for example the primary-source stock identified in `sources.json`. Ordered diaphragm coupons are 0.004 in **0.1016 mm**, with a 0.010 in **0.2540 mm** comparator. Measure thickness at multiple locations with suitable instrumentation; ±0.01 mm would be a large stiffness uncertainty for the thin coupon. The receipt uses nominal E = 200 GPa for stainless and 69 GPa for aluminium as explicitly assumed screening values. Obtain alloy/temper identification and a suitable material certificate rather than treating those values as supplied minimum strength. The 100 MPa leaf stress screen is a deliberately declared design screen, not a manufacturer's yield/fatigue rating.

The basic tools are a drill/mill with depth stops, suitable small taps/reamers, a deburring method that does not notch thin spring edges, a way to cut foil without distortion, and a lathe or purchased equivalent for the captive fine-pitch screw journal. Laser-cut spring/foil edges require inspection and finishing; heat-affected edges are not automatically acceptable. Wet-cell joining is a separate qualified polymer process. The envelope model does not specify a solvent chemistry or make a transparent tank pressure-rated.

## Fixed structure and actual string load path

Fasten two 60 × 100 × 8 anchor feet at global X = 15…75 and 540…600, Y = 22…122, Z = 12…20. Their four M5 base-joint hole centres are X = 25/65 and 550/590 respectively, each at Y = 36/108. Use through-bolts, washers and nuts rather than relying on a thread in a thin base. The 20 × 80 × 110 webs sit on the feet at X = 35…55 and 560…580, Y = 32…112, Z = 20…130. Two M4 web-to-foot bolts on each centre line use the stated 16 mm tapped engagement; check actual length so they neither bottom nor protrude into another feature. The calculation reports both the M5 base-joint demand and the larger M4 web-joint overturning demand. **Bolt grade, joint preload, thread pull-out, contact pressure, base bending and foot restraint remain to be qualified.** The low calculated web stress does not establish those joint strengths.

Four M4 line-retainer/eye interfaces lie at Y = 54/66/78/90, Z = 124. The installed bearing points must be X = 60 and 560. The 4.5 mm web holes are a **retainer mounting interface**: obtain a rated line retainer compatible with 0.5 mm nylon, set its actual bearing location, then record that location. The rendered M4 cylinders are not a fabricated retainer or a rated knot. Do not replace this with an untested sharp screw edge. Individual tension is provisionally capped at 25 N and the four-string bank at 100 N; supplier line strength, clamping slip and the retained ends must support the intended load with an appropriate independently chosen margin. A guard encloses strings before tensioning. Slow manual screw/nut adjustments tune each end; no fast motorized tuning is specified.

The nominal straight speaking leg rises 1 mm over 400 mm, then falls 1 mm over a 100 mm afterlength. Equal front/back tension produces about **0.753 N total bridge downforce** at the illustrative 60.263 N bank tension and only about −0.0028 N longitudinal bridge force. The end banks, not the clay, take the roughly 60 N longitudinal pull. Saddle friction can create unequal leg tension; the receipt separately screens a 10% mismatch up to approximately 10 N at the 100 N cap. That mismatch must be established or bounded for the installed saddle. Actual string mass per length, end compliance, nylon creep, temperature and bridge motion defeat treating the ideal tension formula as a force measurement.

## Four guided leaves and moving carrier

Bolt the four lower clamp feet at X = 400/520, Y = 52/92. Their fixed posts rise from Z = 18 to 55. The leaf blanks occupy Z = 45…105: 10 mm is clamped at each end, leaving Z = 55…95 free. The lower-post top notch is **6 + t/2 = 6.1524 mm** deep from its front Y face for nominal stock. The leaf plane is centred at each Y datum. A 16 × 6 × 10 loose front jaw and a rear post surface grip the lower 10 mm. Upper front jaws occupy Z = 95…105; the 16 × 6 × 18 upper rear jaws extend to Z = 113 and mount the carrier above them. M3 cross bolts pass through the blade holes at Z = 50 and 100. Use washers, matched clean faces and a recorded tightening procedure. The sheet must be clamped across its broad face, not left bending on a bolt shank.

The 152 × 80 × 6 carrier occupies X = 384…536, Y = 40…120, Z = 113…119. Machine its two **20 × 40 through-windows** at global X = 410…430 and 490…510, Y = 60…100; they are included in the mass budget. Do not enlarge the windows into upper-jaw or spacer lands. The bridge is 12 × 72 × 6 at X = 454…466, Y = 36…108, Z = 119…125. Its blind M3 mounting taps are **4 mm deep**. The four rounded seating grooves place strings at the specified Y values without cutting sharp nylon edges. The tall front and rear clevis brackets attach under the carrier; their top-mount and pin coordinates differ, which is why they have separate C06F/C06R drill rows.

With ideal guided boundaries the ordered leaves give roughly **3.540 kN/m Y stiffness** and 3.810 MN/m X stiffness. At 0.25 mm Y motion their ideal restoring force is about 0.885 N. The leaves also carry moving weight and the small string downforce. The complete installed moving mass must be **weighed and at most 0.350 kg** for this screening case. The known machined aluminium estimate is about 0.270 kg, leaving approximately 0.080 kg for spacers, nuts/bolts, drive, accelerometer, target, wiring traction and the participating clay/rod share. A catalogue exciter mass or an unweighed cable is not zero. If the installed mass exceeds the cap, lighten/review the carrier and regenerate the calculation rather than silently raising the cap.

The receipt includes the guided tip moment, an assumed 0.5 mm force-axis error, vertical compression and conservative strong/weak-axis bending stress. It compares compression with a deliberately conservative cantilever Euler load rather than claiming a coupled flexure eigenanalysis. Nominal ratios exceed the declared fourfold comparison screen, but the upper case has little allowance for unmodelled errors. The assumption of an effectively guided, nearly parallel carrier is checked with two independent displacement readings at separated carrier locations. If their inferred tilt exceeds the declared 1 mrad commissioning target, or any clamp moves permanently, stop and redesign. Readout and rod loads are not qualified by this quasi-static write calculation.

## Clay cartridge and mechanical writing port

The fixed pedestal is 55 × 34 × 69 at X = 432.5…487.5, Y = 55…89, Z = 12…81. Its bottom M4 taps connect to the base, and its top M3 taps connect the lower 48 × 32 × 3 plate at Z = 81…84. The pad is nominally 30 × 30 × 8 at X = 445…475, Y = 57…87, Z = 84…92. The upper plate is Z = 92…95; four 18 mm tubular spacers outside the clay footprint join it to the carrier. Use flush upper-plate fastener heads/appropriate registered studs outside the active pad, not bolts through the clay. Verify lengths and clearances on the actual fastener stack before clamping a specimen.

Machine three shallow **30 × 0.8 × 0.3 grooves parallel to X** at plate-local Y = 8,16,24 on each clay-contact face: top face of the lower plate and **bottom face of the upper plate**. The walls then oppose Y shear. Grooves do not prove zero slip; add visible fiducials on clay and both plates, preserve photographs, and classify any slip/contact evolution as an interface effect. The 8 mm thickness is the gap between contact lands, not the groove bottoms. Record mass, specimen batch, preparation, temperature, residual displacement and time since preparation. Material squeezing or extrusion invalidates constant-area assumptions. A noncontact removable skirt may catch debris without joining the plates.

The front writer uses an inline FUTEK LSB200 **FSH00103, 22.2 N** cell on a guided hand-screw carriage. Its **two M3 × 0.5 threaded load ports are only 2.8 mm deep**. Target 2.2 mm engagement, verified by a depth/length inspection, with a jam nut that does not drive the stud into the bottom. The manufacturer's FI1041-G drawing governs the stepped 17.5 × 19.1 mm body and 6.7/8.0 mm profile. The red transparent SCAD box is a keepout only. **Do not clamp, support or touch the non-loading body surfaces.** Standard vendor calibration is tension; compression requires its own applicable calibration. Both signs and the assembled load path must be calibrated. The cell's nominal 127 µm full-scale compliance implies approximately 29 µm at 5 N and 114 µm at 20 N, so the specimen displacement comes from the independent carrier measurement after the cell, not from screw revolutions.

The cell axis is global X = 460, Z = 92, with its body ports nominally Y = 20.9 and 40. The active-end connector reaches the front clevis pin at (460,48,92); use the manufacturer-marked active end. A small clevis/spherical load connector must transmit signed axial force while relieving assembly-angle error. **The two W07 studs and adapter/yoke blanks are explicitly shop-fit interfaces, not a claim of an exact catalogue rod-end fit.** Inspect the chosen connector's size, free articulation, load rating and side clearance before releasing the adapter drilling. The sensor must not be used to react screw torque. The load line must meet the upper clay contact plane; the 0.5 mm error in the screen is not a manufacturer's permissible side-load specification.

Two 6 mm ground guide rods run along Y from −30 to 35, centred X = 450/470, Z = 72. Four guide posts locate them above the base. Line-ream the carriage bores and align the rods before fixing their clamps; do not force mismatched bores into alignment using the force cell. Nominal 6.05 mm bores are a low-speed shop-fit clearance proposal; verify actual play, friction and freedom. Any guide stick-slip lies upstream of the cell but can still excite the specimen. Lubricants stay away from the clay and water cell.

The writer has a **machined M6 × 0.5 lead screw**, a matched fixed nut/bush, and a captive rotating thrust journal in the carriage front yoke. W04 is a screw blank, **not a sourced complete push-pull actuator**. Turn a 4 mm journal with thrust washers/collars so the screw can rotate while pulling and pushing the carriage; verify axial capture and backlash in both directions. The 50 × 15 × 20 fixed nut beam rests on two legs outside the guide posts. Its thread axis is (X460,Z92). Select and document the actual nut fit and thrust hardware before fabrication; alternatively install a separately specified bidirectional manual stage with a validated adapter. A plain ball-ended micrometer that only pushes does not replace this captive bidirectional mechanism. The 0.5 mm/revolution pitch is command geometry, not measured specimen travel.

The free front pin is removable. For acoustic reading, pull its retainer, remove the pin, retract the carriage **15 mm** and verify a visible gap to both clevis ears. The parked hand knob can project about 20 mm beyond the front base edge. No writer contact or cable should load the free bridge during reads. Slow writing also requires the **rear diaphragm rod disconnected**; the thin membrane is not a ±0.25/0.5 mm travel stop.

Manual force and displacement limits require a **validated live operator display** of calibrated force and after-cell carrier displacement, with recording running. An end-of-file batch receipt cannot enforce a limit while a hand screw is turning. If live display is unavailable, writing is blocked; a separately validated tiny-step/latch/dwell/current-reading procedure may be developed, but this document does not authorize blind turns. Implement measured mechanical catches slightly outside the accepted motion window with a reviewed load path before increasing force; any catch contact terminates the run. The current adapter blank does not provide a qualified catch. Never use the 20 N screen or a vendor overload number as a substitute for an operating limit display and stops.

## Water cell, zero-load rod and optical qualification access

The cell has internal X = 380…540, Y = 160…260, Z = 35…135. Its 172 × 112 × 6 bottom is at Z = 29…35, 172 × 6 × 100 long walls at Y = 154…160 and 260…266, and 6 × 100 × 100 ends at X = 374…380 and 540…546. This gives the stated 160 × 100 × 100 interior. The membrane aperture is 40 mm in the near wall, centred (460,154,92). Two 60 mm OD/40 mm ID rings and eight 3.4 mm holes on a 50 mm pitch circle sandwich the foil and compatible gaskets. Use a controlled, recorded tightening sequence selected for the polymer, gaskets and ring flatness. The model's free-stack Y positions are nominal; actual compressed gasket thickness and wet membrane position must be measured. The comparator thickness changes the stack and requires a fresh zero, calibration and geometry identifier.

The 8 mm dry centre boss is bonded to the **dry** coupon face. It does not puncture the foil. Bond chemistry, cure, creep and cyclic life require a separate coupon test; the eight perimeter bolts do not qualify the centre bond. The rod is a fitted 3 mm member with an adjustable length and indexed collar, detachable at the rear pin (460,110,92). Fill to Z = 115 (80 mm depth, nominal 1.28 L) with a splash lid that visibly vents to atmosphere. Verify the no-contact diaphragm wet position before setting the rod length to **near-zero static force/preload**. The ideal thin-plate sag is about 29 µm for the ordered thin coupon at 23 mm water head; it is an estimate about an unknown actual membrane state, not a specified assembly displacement. Never shorten the rod to pull that sag back to its dry position.

Provide an unobstructed optical line along +Y to a high-contrast point on the dry boss annulus, for example X = 462.5, Z = 92, avoiding the central 3 mm rod. A stationary removable optical head mount on the base/tank frame can be adjusted to the selected sensor's working distance; it must not ride on the bridge. This qualification port adds no massive moving dial indicator. It supports a **high-rate independent displacement measurement at the diaphragm boss** or an independently validated rigid-rod kinematic transfer. The 50 Hz slow laser at the carrier cannot by itself bound audio-frequency diaphragm motion, and acceleration at a different carrier point is not automatically the same motion. Probe operation remains blocked until the actual ≤10 µm diaphragm-motion limit is verified under the installed wet load, or a valid conservative conversion from the selected dynamic sensor is established. A fixed digital command amplitude supplies neither result.

Suspend the 25 × 46 mm hydrophone vertically, centred (500,215,80), giving body Z = 57…103. Its cable support is fixed to the lid/frame with slack that cannot rub the walls, boss or rod. Keep all connector heads dry. The reflective subwavelength tank measures a coupled structure/water transfer; it is not an anechoic acoustic calibrator or an independent pressure/force standard. Electronics remain on the separate dry rack described by the electronics workstream; the old complete-instrument cost allowance does not quote that rack.

## Commissioning and acceptance record

Before installing clay or tensioning strings, inspect actual machined dimensions, leaf thickness/free span, parallelism, tap depths, fastener seating and every clearance. Record the installed moving mass. Qualify clamp retention and the fixture without assuming a material law. Compare force against two measured carrier displacement locations to detect rotation. An accepted nominal calculation receipt is only a dimensional/arithmetic check; it cannot replace these measurements. All calibration records name M03, its parameter hash, the actual parts and sensors, fixture state and software commits.

Begin with no specimen/appropriate registered rigid reference and the writer pin engaged, rear rod removed. Confirm signed force calibration in tension and compression, after-cell displacement, live display, zero/tare history, frame compliance and slow time alignment. A registered spacer does not mean infinite fixture stiffness. Use the measured small motions to establish an initial local operating envelope. Increments stop on **either** force or displacement, any interface slip, tilt, clamp movement, force-cell side contact or unexpected preload. Zeroing away a residual force does not erase physical stored state; preserve raw DC baseline.

Leak-test the cell at atmospheric head in a catch tray before attaching the rod or putting electronics nearby. Compare rod out, rod attached/empty, and rod attached/filled at a fixed depth. Measure wet equilibrium, reconnection repeatability, static tangent response and relevant dynamic transfer with the actual sensor positions. If fluid/diaphragm loading dominates the cartridge sensitivity, revise the coupling rather than inferring material parameters from the hydrophone alone. Remove any contact indicator before free acoustic measurement unless its added dynamics are deliberately part of calibration.

Only then prepare independent clay and elastomer/rigid controls with fixed final geometry, controlled histories and predefined waits. Preserve the slow baseline and raw waveforms. Compare histories across cartridges/remounts and record detach/reattach operations and manual retunes as state-changing events. A repeatable difference is a signature; retention and useful computation each require their own matched controls. No result in this folder establishes plastic memory, a constitutive law, useful computation or a tenfold gain.

Fabrication review is complete only after the **connector/yoke/nut/catch details, source material, tank bond, centre-boss bond, string retainers and guard** have been inspected and released by the builder. These conditional interfaces are named openly rather than disguised as qualified commercial parts. No hardware driver, purchase, calibration or physical operation has been performed by these files.
