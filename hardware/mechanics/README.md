# M03 manual mechanical build

This is a dimensioned prototype revision for fabrication review. No object has been built, no material law has been measured, and the calculations below do not qualify a machine. The original `design/fusion_design` packet remains unchanged. M03 makes its force paths and interfaces explicit, including the parts that must be checked before fabrication proceeds.

Run `python hardware/mechanics/build.py` to regenerate the parameter include, cut/drill tables and load-calculation receipt. Open `assembly.scad` in OpenSCAD; `part="assembly"`, `part="leaf"`, `part="clay_plate"`, `part="diaphragm_ring"` and `part="carrier"` select assembly or individual manufacturing parts. `generated/fabrication.svg` is the coordinate drawing. All local dimensions are millimetres. Tapped holes are represented by their pilot diameter; the drill table, not the rendered thread-free cylinder, specifies the thread. Commercial sensors and fasteners are identified as envelopes rather than invented detailed vendor CAD.

The base grows from 600 × 320 to **600 × 360 × 12**, with Y = −40…320, to support a manual writer. Its parked hand screw can project a further 20 mm beyond the front base edge. The four flexures move to X = 400/520, Y = 52/92, leaving room for cartridge fastening and eliminating the old post/anchor overlap. The speaking nut stays at X = 60 and bridge at X = 460; the afterlength anchor moves to X = 560. The upper clay contact, force sensor and diaphragm rod share Z = 92. The carrier rises to the string contact at Z = 125. This is one shared clay-sensitive transverse bridge coordinate, not four independent memory cells.

The first manual write envelope is **±5 N or ±0.25 mm, whichever is reached first**, with the diaphragm rod removed. The ±20 N/±0.5 mm case is a conditional engineering screening case, not baseline permission: qualify flexures, material, clamps, stops, force calibration and slip first. The 22.2 N cell operates close to rated load at 20 N; its overload specification is not a reason to use that level routinely. Acoustic reads begin at **at most 10 µm measured dynamic diaphragm displacement about wet equilibrium**, with no writer contact. A normalized digital amplitude does not enforce that displacement limit. There is no motorized writing or tuning in M03.

String end banks carry longitudinal tension into the base. Equal front/back tension largely cancels at the bridge; its remaining force is a small longitudinal difference and the downward resultant from the two break angles. The four guided leaves carry the moving mass and that downward force. The clay pad is sheared along Y between a fixed pedestal and the moving upper plate. Its keyed faces reduce gross interface slip without proving adhesion or a bulk constitutive law. The writer force cell lies in series between a guided hand-screw carriage and a removable front clevis pin; force and displacement must be measured after screw compliance and cell deflection. The rear rod has its own removable clevis, adjustable zero-load length and a bonded dry diaphragm boss. It never pierces the water boundary.

Detailed fabrication, assembly, calibration and acceptance instructions are in `ASSEMBLY.md`. `sources.json` distinguishes primary product dimensions from assumed material properties and engineering screening limits. This revision gives a machinist a coordinate table and explicit parts, while preserving the outstanding qualification steps; it is not a certified pressure vessel, fatigue design or ready-to-run controller.
