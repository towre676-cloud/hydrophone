# Commissioning and calibration interface

**This directory prepares physical measurements; it contains no completed physical calibration or trial.** The released mechanics and electronics drawings govern assembly and limits. `calibration_record.template.json` is deliberately disabled. `calibration_record.synthetic_example.json` is an arithmetic test fixture carrying `synthetic_demo` throughout conversion. Neither nominal manufacturer sensitivities nor a successful software replay changes those labels. The other research session owns the task, acceptable-action sets, retention claim and preregistered success thresholds; this workstream supplies measurement conditions and uncertainty interfaces.

**What the present recording measures.** `instrument.analyze.analyze_tones` jointly fits known cosines and sines, DC and centered trend over one stationary common-clock window. Its outputs are complex digital channel ratios. A drive electrical loopback removes a particular sampled electrical-input factor; it does not measure force transmitted by the exciter to the bridge. A hydrophone/direct-accelerometer ratio can be a useful boundary-transmission signature but becomes Pa/(m/s^2) only after both chains have appropriate calibration. Pa/N requires a separate measurement of actual dynamic force at the structure-side coupling. The slow writer load cell measures a different port and cannot supply the audio-force denominator. This distinction follows established modal-test practice; the manufacturer explanation is recorded in `sources.json`.

**G0: identify the whole acquisition chain.** Assign configuration, calibration, sensor-serial, mount, cable, conditioning, gain-setting, audio-clock and software-version identifiers before connecting the exciter. Record polarity, axes, hydrophone coordinates/orientation, water depth and temperature, room temperature, rod clevis/zero-load collar state, string tuning, cartridge batch/mass/geometry, bridge preload and elapsed time since writing. Preserve native data and all zero readings. The current tone fitter accepts PCM16; if hardware records another format, preserve that original and document a reproducible conversion, full-scale convention, quantization and new hash. Calibrate the same complete conversion pipeline. Disable AGC, normalization, limiting and automatic gain changes. A manifest statement that a clock or gain is fixed is not its measurement.

**G1: establish an electrical baseline before mechanical excitation.** With the exciter disconnected, use only the electronics workstream's approved isolated/attenuated loopback connection. Route one known electrical signal into each intended recording path, including the actual line-input load and cable, and measure relative gain, phase, polarity, crosstalk, noise, rail codes, overload recovery and drift at the planned tone frequencies. Exchange channels/cables while retaining labels to reveal channel dependence. Measure absolute input volts against an independent calibrated reference if V-based transfers are required; explicitly convert reference RMS readings to the peak phasor convention. A loopback gain curve alone cannot establish the hydrophone's acoustic sensitivity or the accelerometer's mechanical sensitivity. Record warm-up and settling observations rather than assuming an arbitrary delay is sufficient.

**G2: commission the dry mechanical path with the rod disconnected.** Check indexed motion and sensor-mount security against the released M03 drawings. The mechanics draft begins manual writer commissioning at the first of 5 N or 0.25 mm, with the water rod disconnected; its possible later 20 N/0.5 mm envelope is conditional, not released operating permission. The proposed reader limit is 10 micrometres of dynamic diaphragm/boss displacement about wet equilibrium and must be qualified at that mechanical port, not inferred from DAC amplitude or slow writer travel. Use a rigid cartridge first, then the reference viscoelastic insert. Observe bridge motion along the declared sensing axis, fixture compliance, unwanted modes, rubbing and preload changes. Accelerometer mass and mounting are part of the configuration; the selected PCB 352C33 alone is 5.8 g according to its manufacturer. Optical fiducials allow a separate displacement check. Do not put a massless sensor into the digital twin when the physical sensor is attached.

**G2/G3 release gate: dynamic diaphragm motion is still unqualified.** A 50 scans/s writer-position log cannot observe the musical-band diaphragm stroke, and a carrier accelerometer is on another mechanical port. Before attaching the final read drive, measure diaphragm/boss motion with a dynamically calibrated optical/vibration instrument, or independently validate the bridge-to-diaphragm translation/rotation transfer and its uncertainty over the intended amplitude/frequency/wet-preload domain. M03 must provide optical access at the boss. Repositioning the existing laser to an indexed boss target and increasing ADC rate is only a candidate qualification setup: its bandwidth, installed linearity and local error must still be demonstrated. The 10 mm/12-bit output has a nominal 2.44 micrometre code and the quoted full-range error is comparable to the 10 micrometre ceiling; neither a higher sample rate nor averaging proves sufficient local accuracy. Until that independent qualification exists, the amplitude setting is a bench-checkout command and the 10 micrometre ceiling is a release block, not an enforced displacement measurement.

**G3: qualify the water path by controlled comparisons.** Keep the tank vented and use the mechanics-approved fill mark. Run the matched condition matrix with tank empty/water, rod out/in, and cartridge absent/rigid dummy/reference viscoelastic/clay; eliminate invalid combinations by a recorded guard rather than silently omitting them. Record hydrophone position, orientation, cable restraint, bubbles, free-surface level, diaphragm and gasket revision, temperatures and the rod's zero-load engagement for every condition. Empty-versus-water changes mass/loading, and rod engagement changes boundary conditions, so their difference is not automatically a clay effect. A rod-out run measures electrical, air and structural bypass paths while retaining the same intended non-contact geometry. Repeating it after rod-in runs checks drift. This small water cell is not assumed to contain a far-field progressive wave; neither p=rho*c*v at the bridge nor acoustic power from p^2/(rho*c) is justified by one hydrophone reading.

**Frequency and stationary-window qualification.** Select every test frequency inside the measured end-to-end band of every channel needed for that transfer. The software score's C4/D4/E4/G4 targets are musical targets, not calibration evidence. The illustrative model's 65-240 Hz band is also not an established device band. Begin with isolated known tones and a low-level swept survey, then use the chosen fixed multitone grid after stationary response has been checked. Acquire pre-drive, settling, stationary and post-drive regions separately; fit only the declared stationary window. Compare two non-overlapping stationary windows and repeated windows, inspect residual spectra/omitted harmonics separately, and repeat at at least three measured reader amplitudes within the released displacement limit to examine local linearity and read disturbance. A small least-squares residual cannot prove absence of bias or provide a deterministic noise bound. A nearly singular joint basis is rejected by the existing fitter rather than interpreted physically.

**Phase and clocks.** Audio phasors share the selected window's time origin; cross-channel phase needs common sampling and an independently measured channel-skew bound. A timing error dt produces phase error 2*pi*f*dt radians. Allocate a phase budget before selecting a required skew tolerance, then measure it with the electrical loopback and include drift/filters. Do not infer phase from a magnitude-only sensitivity sheet. Slow force/displacement loggers may use a separate clock; retain original timestamps and establish trigger offset, drift and resampling error explicitly. Audio/slow phase relationships are unavailable until that clock mapping is qualified.

**A shared slow ADC clock is not simultaneous sampling or filter correction.** For a sequential T7 scan, channel i has an epoch t_scan(n)+delta_i; preserve the scan order and each channel offset. The current candidate spacing is approximately 670 microseconds, so force, displacement and marker epochs differ. The main E01 candidate uses DC-coupled 5 Hz four-pole Butterworth paths for force and position, while the physical marker is read unfiltered. An ideal such low-pass has roughly 83 ms low-frequency group delay; the installed delay/gain must be measured. The marker clock map estimates clocks, not those signal-path transfer functions. A filter delay is frequency-dependent and generally cannot be removed by subtracting one constant offset. Either characterize and apply an independently justified channel-transfer/time reconstruction with uncertainty, or restrict readings to demonstrated settled holds and bounded slow trajectories. Native raw time/voltage records and the filter model/settings remain necessary; no automatic debiasing is claimed by the sealed-run pipeline.

**ADC fit frequency and SI frequency differ.** The seal code correctly maps DAC tones into the ADC nominal-time axis using the relative DAC-to-ADC drift. This does not calibrate either clock against the SI second. If nominal ADC time equals k times SI time, then physical frequency equals k times the fitted ADC-nominal frequency. The SI reference utility now requires an independent k and its declared error, and checks that its resulting physical-frequency interval lies within each sensitivity point's declared frequency domain. The calibration points are explicitly on an SI-Hz axis. There is no silent interpolation, and no assumption that a sample-rate label is an absolute frequency calibration. Non-affine or within-window clock variation can also bias stationary phasors; its effect belongs in the independently supplied phasor/calibration error budgets.

**End-to-end sensitivity and present parts.** For channel j, define S_j(f)=digital full-scale phasor / physical SI phasor, including sensor, supply, conditioning, load, cable, ADC gain and any digital conversion. Then x_j=y_j/S_j and x_r/x_d=(y_r/y_d)*(S_d/S_r). Each sensitivity is complex and frequency-specific. The H2dX manual gives a typical -165 dB re 1 V/micropascal response under specified phantom-power conditions and explains dependence on preamp bias/load/cable, without detailed response plots. The corresponding 5.62 mV/Pa is a nominal design calculation, not a calibrated pressure scale. PCB's 352C33 is specified as a 100 mV/g accelerometer; it does not measure force. PCB explicitly notes that the 480E09's unbuffered output and receiving load affect its low-frequency behavior. Therefore those nominal specifications do not enable the conversion template. Obtain a scoped low-frequency hydrophone magnitude/phase calibration or comparison against an independently calibrated reference under suitable conditions; NPL describes closed-chamber, coupler-reciprocity and low-frequency free-field methods. Its existence does not calibrate this instrument, and its listed ultrasound service must not be extrapolated down to the musical band.

**G4: qualify the slow writer separately.** With rod disconnected, retain raw load-cell/position counts, excitation/reference voltage where applicable, original timestamps and the independent reference used to determine SI scale. Calibrate installed positive and negative force over the intended commissioning range using several known references, approach/release cycles and repeated zeros. Mass references need the documented gravity convention and alignment; a static scale does not establish dynamic audio-force sensitivity. Calibrate displacement over the intended stroke against an independent dimensional reference, recording resolution, nonlinearity, backlash, thermal drift and fixture compliance. First run no-load and rigid-dummy approach/hold/release sequences, then reference viscoelastic and clay cartridges. Keep force and position baselines before and after each run; do not continually retare away creep or preload. The template separately records raw, zero and scale uncertainty, plus missing linearity/hysteresis/temperature/creep contributions. An affine conversion with three assigned bounds does not cover those missing contributions unless the assigned bounds already include them.

**Measured slow work and geometry.** The writer port's sampled integral F dx is port work. It includes possible elastic storage, fixture compliance, friction, coupling and dissipation elsewhere. It is not isolated clay heat or clay-only dissipation. A closed plotted loop is not sufficient evidence of energy loss if position or force zero drift is uncontrolled; retain the raw closure mismatch, baseline differences and time history. The existing slow module provides conditional bounds only for the sampled trapezoidal path and declared measurement errors, leaving unobserved intersample motion unbounded. Clay strain requires a separately validated deformation/compliance map. The optical fiducials and indexed M03 force line at Z=92 mm support that later map; they do not supply it automatically.

**G5: distinguish written history from readout changes.** Interleave rigid/reference/dummy checks with the clay history protocol, preserve independent geometry/mass/preload observations, and record repeated reads immediately before and after each write at matched temperature and elapsed time. Deliberately change a recording gain in a labelled control run, then restore and recheck it. Compare hydrophone/drive, direct-vibration/drive and hydrophone/direct-vibration together. A pure hydrophone gain change can resemble a change in the water path, so these ratios alone do not uniquely separate the two; a sensor-chain check independent of the clay is still required. An electrical injection bypasses the acoustic sensor and cannot diagnose every acoustic-sensitivity drift. Sensor replacement or dismount/remount is a new configuration unless reproducibility is demonstrated. The task owner must decide whether visible changed geometry is the intended persistent configuration or a nuisance to be matched when testing hidden history.

**A model boundary that must survive commissioning.** For a fixed-coefficient standard linear solid, the incremental frequency response does not depend on the initial internal relaxation coordinate. Different initial histories can therefore give the same acoustic FRF. Its internal state relaxes, so this model does not establish retained or nonvolatile memory. A detectable written state must change some measured coefficient, contact, geometry, nonlinear operating point, or other observable state; that requires experiments. Neither physical calibration nor a decision-solver certificate fills this missing physical link.

**Files and executable scope.** `experiment_interface.json` lists mandatory identity, condition and channel fields, the staged comparison matrix and the boundary to the other session's success criteria. `calibration_record.schema.json` describes records; the reference implementation adds runtime semantic checks. `si_conversion.py` rejects disabled templates, wrong configuration/rate/temperature, missing phase, mismatched quantity/unit/role, missing exact frequency points, zero sensitivities and unresolved denominator uncertainty. It performs no interpolation or extrapolation and requires an independently declared ADC-to-SI timebase factor/error before matching SI-frequency sensitivities. It never substitutes a residual RMS or a statistical expanded uncertainty for a deterministic error disk. Missing input-error bounds yield nominal SI values without a deterministic bound. Calibration evidence remains a declaration whose authenticity is not verified by this module, and environment, amplitude, ageing, mounting and clock scope beyond explicitly checked fields still require the commissioning record. The module is an opt-in reference utility; the root acquisition code does not silently enable pressure conversion.

**Exact-decimal uncertainty arithmetic.** For nominal complex y and sensitivity S, xhat=y/S. If independently supplied deterministic disks give |y_true-y|<=r_y and |S_true-S|<=r_S, the code uses the conservative rational lower bound L=max(|Re S|,|Im S|)-r_S and rejects L<=0. Its exact-decimal disk radius is [r_y+(|Re xhat|+|Im xhat|)*r_S]/L. This contains the complex-ratio error under those declared inputs, while printed amplitudes/phases are approximate display arithmetic. Decimal inputs represent the declared numerical model, not a claim to have measured exact physical quantities. For slow x=(raw-zero)*scale, interval corners give an exact rectangular enclosure of the supplied three uncertainties. None of this adds unknown calibration sources or certifies a physical plant.

**Verification.** Run `python -m unittest discover -s hardware/calibration -p test_si_conversion.py -v` from the repository root. Tests use synthetic numbers and check complex conversion, exact perturbation containment, genuine force-role requirements, disabled/invalid calibration rejection, statistical-versus-deterministic uncertainty separation, and affine slow-sensor interval arithmetic. The blocked template remains the physical starting point until a real calibration record and its raw evidence exist.
