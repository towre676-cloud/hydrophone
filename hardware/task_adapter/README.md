# Raw measurements to the clay-history tuning task

`instrument/task_features.py` connects the physical raw-record format to the shared `experiments/clay_tuning_v1/contract.json` feature and outcome fields. It does not estimate natural string pitch, perform physical calibration, create qualification evidence, select an action, or infer a useful material-memory effect. Its output is **offline replay**, not proof that a decision was computed on the target computer before the five-second deadline. Input identities and bounds remain independently supplied declarations whose laboratory authenticity is not verified by code.

Use `instrument.episode.compile_episode_commands` to create the actual 21-second stereo full and reduced command files. The full four-tone source occupies 2–4 seconds; reduced commands are silent. The final second is recording-support padding and must appear in the episode/reset cost ledger, not disappear from measured wall time. The command hashes to register are the complete files actually played, not the two-second mathematical source. The manual writer-release and rod-engagement transitions must be qualified before the declared time-zero pluck; they cannot be assumed instantaneous. A completed `mode_transition` declaration must include its validation identity, scope, final writer/rod states, and an upper completion time including settling no later than zero. Physical runs also require hash-bound actual event/timing evidence. Missing qualification, late settling or a rod state incompatible with the assigned condition rejects extraction. If they cannot fit the fixed history/rest schedule, revise and register a new core before confirmation. A canonical raw run must retain the compiler's `probe.json`, `source_probe.wav` and `experiment_core.json` beside its command. The adapter replays the source renderer and the complete command timeline before extracting data. The episode request must contain every nonnegative core cost component and at least one second of reset cost; its exact normalized ledger is preserved in the pre-feature record and must be copied unchanged into the final confirmation row.

**Current M04 physical binding is blocked for the unchanged v1 force feature.** Its force cell belongs to the removable writer. Once that writer is disconnected for the time-zero pluck and free acoustic read, its later force channel is an unloaded writer baseline, not a clay-reaction or relaxation measurement. This adapter rejects such a physical port for the v1 1–2 second force feature. It accepts physical force only from a separately qualified permanent clay-reaction sensor, with serial, mount, connection state, evidence identities and exactly matching hash-bound operator configuration. M04 does not contain that additional port. A versioned timing amendment with a measured write-to-read transfer interval is a distinct route for the mathematical workstream; this adapter does not silently alter the registered core. Synthetic plumbing explicitly labels its nonphysical reaction trace and cannot promote it to a physical reading.

The required inputs are a hash-bound `fusion.run/1`, the exact experiment core, a completed per-run qualification record, a separate free-decay pitch record, and an episode request. `qualification.template.json` is disabled and contains no physical bounds. Complete it with independently supported installed calibration, synchronization, time pairing, temperature, probe detection and pitch qualification; bind raw run and source revision hashes. Do not replace missing evidence with nominal manufacturer sensitivity, regression residuals or synthetic IDs. Physical qualification must include actual hash-bound evidence files. Its configuration, mechanics and electronics identities must match both the canonical run and its hash-bound operator setup record; a M03 calibration cannot silently become an M04 setup record. Code checks identities, declared scope and consistency; a qualifying filename is not a measurement.

The per-run episode clock declares `audio_time = audio_time_at_write_end + scale * episode_time`. It must cover the whole task, agree with the actual DAC/ADC relation at the 2–4 second slot within the registered clock allowance, and keep every selected window plus its timing allowance inside actual raw audio. A default 21-second capture supports the outcome window ending at 20 seconds when the epoch and uncertainty fit that support. A large positive epoch offset may require additional recording support; the adapter rejects it rather than inventing samples. Reduced silence cannot establish a new waveform clock map by correlation; its common-device clock relation still requires independent qualification.

Slow force, displacement and room temperature come from the calibrated derivative CSV, retaining `logger_time_s,force_N,displacement_m,temperature_C`. Native voltages/counts and their conversion evidence remain in the run. The existing four-channel T7 wiring does not measure room temperature: supply an independently qualified room sensor and timing conversion, rather than populating physical data with 20 degrees. Channel-specific time corrections and complete error bounds must cover sequential sampling, physical filters and clock/epoch errors. A marker clock map does not correct an approximately 83 ms slow-filter delay. The adapter requires a qualified reconstructed-time scope and records every selected source row; this declaration does not itself validate a deconvolution or a constant-delay approximation.

Temperature and position use samples in [1,2) seconds. The force feature uses [1.8,2.0); relaxation is mean force in [1.0,1.2) minus mean force in [1.8,2.0). Means are exact rational **arithmetic sample means** of supplied decimal/rational CSV values, not continuous-time integrals or an automatic uniform-domain error certificate. This extractor choice must be bound before development fitting; changing mean definitions requires a new extractor registration. Sample gaps and actual window support are checked. Error in continuous-time mean, between-sample motion, reconstructed values or calibration still belongs to the independently supplied qualification.

The electrical probe is jointly fitted at all four frequencies in [2.1,3.9) seconds on the actual ADC nominal-time axis. DAC/ADC drift adjusts the fitted frequencies. The full arm exports E4 bridge/observed-loopback and water/observed-loopback amplitude ratios. These remain digital ratios at fixed registered gains; they are not Pa/N or a clay modulus. The reduced arm exports neither probe feature. Both arms check actual electrical presence or omission at all four tones using independently qualified amplitude-error and threshold declarations, allowing a zero drive coefficient in the omitted case. Correlation or least-squares residuals never supply these bounds silently.

Reduced feature values end at 2 seconds, but this concrete extractor's electrical omission audit ends at 4 seconds. Its request therefore declares an actual decision time strictly later than `4 + 2*clock_error` and no later than the core's five-second deadline. It writes `feature_end_s` separately from `pre_action_quality_window_end_s`. This does not establish reduced decision availability at 2 seconds or a wall-clock saving. Target-computer execution time and the actual decision event need separately logged prospective qualification. Rejected or incomplete episodes must remain assigned operational failures in the evaluator's failure ledger, not be discarded after seeing outcomes.

Natural pitch is a separate evidence input. `pitch_record.template.json` requires an independently qualified E4 **free-decay pluck**, SI pitch, a complete pitch error no greater than the task's one-cent ceiling, ADC/estimator/nonstationarity scope, actual source audio hash, channel and exact frame window. Pre-action uses 0.10–1.00 seconds with a pluck at zero; outcome uses 18.10–20.00 seconds with a separate pluck at 18. A driven sinusoid at the target is not a natural-pitch measurement. The code converts the supplied qualified pitch to cents using an exact rational enclosure of the logarithm: range reduction to [1,2), a positive atanh series with a geometric remainder bound, and outward rounding to a compact decimal. Its numerical conversion allowance is added to the externally supplied pitch error before checking the one-cent ceiling. It does not certify the estimator or invent an error bound from a numerical fit.

An independent manual-action record supplies achieved cents and its error, with evidence during 5–17 seconds, exact raw frame indices and its own identity. It cannot reuse future outcome pitch to prove action achievement. The outcome adapter packages the independent future pitch and action evidence into the fields checked by `evaluation.evaluate`; the evaluator still determines action failure, pitch success, block independence and the final scientific verdict.

Python entry points from the repository root are `extractor_revision()`, `extract_features(run_path, core_path, qualification_path, pitch_pre_path, episode_request_path, new_output_dir)` and `extract_outcome(run_path, core_path, qualification_path, pitch_post_path, action_record_path, new_output_dir)`. Each successful extraction writes one hashable JSON record with raw source indices, replay diagnostics and provenance. The APIs never overwrite an existing output directory and never access a device. The root CLI may expose these functions without changing their evidence boundary. Tests use explicitly synthetic pitch and qualification declarations to exercise file plumbing, ratios, clocks, omission rejection and outcome separation; they establish no material or physical result.

The extractor also audits the complete raw archive, including the later outcome. This full-file quality decision is available only after capture. `pre_action_quality_window_end_s` identifies the local probe/omission window boundary, not completion time of that archive audit. A future recording failure must remain an assigned operational failure; it must not be used to retrospectively erase a trial or fabricate a past policy decision. Prospective operation needs a separate timestamped prefix/decision log.
