FUSION — CERTIFIED DECISIONS FROM INCOMPLETE OBSERVATIONS
24 October 2026

Open Fusion_Research.html in a browser for the research synthesis and
interactive exact-model illustration. The HTML is self-contained.

Run the independent executable replay with Python 3.10 or newer:

    python fusion_engine.py --out results.json

No additional packages are required. results.json stores exact fractions
as strings. The replay recomputes it from definitions in the script.

Expected checks: 600 panel/noise comparisons between the hyperedge solver
and separate interval-arrangement enumeration; eight three-state minimal
obstructions; optimal declared costs 1,3,7 for action/task/state; failure
at closed noise radius 1/2; exact archived music Gram determinant52002816.

The models are synthetic. The costs are declared illustrative units.
No physical trials or real-world speedup were measured. This synthesis
credits functional observability, Decision Region Determination,
combinatorial Hodge theory, and finite Helly theory as established work.

The fixed-panel interval result assumes independent rectangular possible
readings and fixed acceptable-action sets. State-changing probes need
reset assumptions or complete history-dependent protocols. Missing
hypotheses and invalid calibration invalidate a physical application.

The existing DEFORM source files and historical music files were read,
not modified. Code and proofs in this package are a new research replay.
